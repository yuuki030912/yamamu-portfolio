All 105 Spocco Photo Locations (Nintendo Switch Sports Resort)

By Yamamu Updated October 23, 2026 Nintendo Switch 2 日本語版はこちら

TL;DR: there are 105 Spocco Photos, and this guide shows where every one of them is, No.001 to No.105, using the game's own map screen. Missing a few and can't work out where they are? Tap the number in the list below and you'll jump straight to that location. Every screenshot was taken from Yamamu's own save after completing the full 105/105.

💡 Screenshots are from the Japanese version of the game. The English names here are our own translations (the Japanese name is shown in brackets), so the official English names may differ. The numbers are the same in every language, so match by number.

2. How to read the maps

  • The red frame is the photo point: when you select an entry in the list, the map scrolls to it and the photo icon lights up with a red frame
  • The list on the left shows the number: the selected entry has an arrow and a red underline
  • The panel at the bottom shows the photo itself: the small picture on the left of the panel shows the view you need to capture

FAQ

Q. How many Spocco Photos are there?

There are 105 in total. The Spocco Photo tab in the Island Records menu shows your progress as "xx/105".

Q. How do I find where a Spocco Photo is taken?

In the Island Records Spocco Photo list, select an entry and the map on the right scrolls to that spot, highlighting the photo point with a red frame. Every image in this guide is that screen.

Q. Are the English names official?

No. The names in this guide are our own translations of the Japanese in-game names, which are shown in brackets. The game's English version may use different names, so match by number.
